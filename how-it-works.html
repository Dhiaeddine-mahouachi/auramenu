THE GUEST EXPERIENCE

Scan. Browse. Choose.

Your restaurant menu, available in the browser. Clear categories, readable prices and your own identity.

Explore templates
01

Choose your look

Preview the menu templates, then choose the one that fits your restaurant, café or bar. Start with your own content or request a custom design from AuraDigital.

02

Build your menu

Add categories, dishes, descriptions, prices and photos. Set your restaurant identity and preview the guest experience before submitting.

03

Submit and confirm payment

Choose a plan and submit your request. Your menu stays in progress until payment is verified and AuraDigital approves activation. Follow the bank transfer instructions shown after submission.

04

Share and keep it current

Once approved, share the live menu link and QR code. Sign in to your dashboard to manage your menu while your plan or hosting is active.

Your draft is not a live menu.
A preview helps you check the design. Payment verification and activation happen before guests can access the published menu.
