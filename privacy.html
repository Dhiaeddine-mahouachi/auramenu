Last updated: [date]. This notice covers restaurant owners and staff using AuraMenu (auramenu.space) and AuraMenu Studio, and guests viewing menus.

1. Controller

AuraMenu is provided by AuraDigital. [Legal entity name, address, registration/tax number and contact email — owner to complete].

2. Data we process

  • Account: name, email, a bcrypt hash of your password; for Google sign-in, your Google account ID and email; email verification status. This is the shared AuraDigital account (the same account in AuraMenu, AuraHub and other Aura products).
  • Restaurant and menu content: business name, tagline, address, phone/WhatsApp, opening hours, categories, items, prices, allergen and ingredient information, photos, logo, video links, design settings and published menu versions.
  • Contact and payment records: contact phone, chosen plan, amount, payment status and paid-through date. Payments are made by bank transfer; we do not collect card details.
  • Team: the account email and role (manager/editor) of people added to a menu, and a log of team changes.
  • Drafts: the menu builder draft, saved to your account when you are signed in.
  • Technical data: IP address (rate limiting and security logs; emails, passwords and link tokens are not logged), language/theme preferences stored in your browser, an offline copy of menus.
  • Guests: no account is needed to view a menu. A WhatsApp order message is composed on the guest's device and sent by the guest through WhatsApp.

3. Purposes

Creating and securing your account; building, publishing and hosting menus; confirming payment and activating plans; enforcing team permissions; sending verification and password-reset emails; keeping the service secure.
